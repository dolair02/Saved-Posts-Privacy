Privacy Policy for Saved Posts

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Saved Posts (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) is a mobile application that helps users organize and manage saved social media posts.

Information We Collect

Saved Posts does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to external servers. All data you create (saved posts, notes, tags, folders, and reminders) is stored locally on your device only.

How We Use Information

We do not collect any personal data. The app works completely offline and does not require an account.

Data Storage

All your content remains on your device. We do not have access to it.

Third-Party Services

The app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children’s Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the app or on this page.
